\chapter{Prioritized Remediation Roadmap}
\label{ch:remediation}

This roadmap groups the findings into three priority tiers based on exploitability, attack
complexity, authentication requirements, and business impact. The ordering within each tier
follows the principle of \textit{fix the key that opens all the other doors first}.

\begin{warningbox}
  \textbf{Before starting any remediation:} \fillme{If any finding exposed a live credential,
  say so here and cross-reference it -- rotation should happen before or alongside every other
  fix, regardless of completion order.}
\end{warningbox}
